# Grill Me

Read [grilling](../grilling/workflow.md) and follow its interview workflow.

Keep the review focused on the user's stated scope. Establish available facts yourself, then ask about unresolved decisions and assumptions. If the user has already supplied the necessary decisions, review the approach and report the findings without inventing questions.

When the user requests both review and execution, preserve that requested outcome and existing authorization; continue with the authorized work after material uncertainties are resolved.
